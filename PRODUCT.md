## Stack

## Users

Anyone looking for a medical deep tissue massage ("everyone", per the user, no narrower segment defined). Most will arrive from Instagram on a phone. Her Instagram bio shows a sports/training thread (🏋🏾‍♀️), which is an observed hint, not a confirmed audience.

## Product Purpose

Present Or Marom's practice and let visitors book an appointment. Success: a visitor understands what she offers, where she works, and books a slot.

## Positioning

Medical massage therapist (מעסה רפואית) working in the Deep Tissue technique, run by one person with a personal voice ("דרך החשיבה שלי" is a highlight on her Instagram).

Clinic location: Hod HaSharon (open now). Tel Aviv: coming soon. Appointments are booked through an online calendar (user's answer). The calendar link has not been provided yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer: medical massage, Deep Tissue technique.
- Booking: online calendar, URL pending. Until provided, the booking button points to her Instagram profile and must be swapped.
- No payments, no publishing, until the user asks.
- Prices, hours, exact address, phone number: not provided. Do not invent.

- Name: אור מרום | Or Marom.
- Bio line: "מעסה רפואית בטכניקת Deep Tissue", "הוד השרון | תל אביב (בקרוב)", "תורים פנויים".

- Atmosphere must match a treatment room: calm, warm, safe.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is always one tap away on mobile.
2. Say only what is confirmed. Empty slots stay visibly marked as pending, never filled with invented proof.
3. Calm over clever: the page should feel like the room.
4. Her own voice and her own media carry the trust, not stock claims.
